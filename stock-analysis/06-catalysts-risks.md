# Phase 6: Catalysts & Risks — What Could Change the Thesis

**Who drives:** LLM (news synthesized from collected sources) + Human

**Purpose:** Identify the concrete **catalysts** that could move the stock and the **risks** that could break the thesis. This directly supports the buy/hold/sell decision.

## Catalysts (upside)
Time-boxed, verifiable, material events that could drive re-rating:
- Upcoming quarterly/annual results and expected drivers
- Product launches / new segments / major orders
- Regulatory approvals / policy tailwinds (HK/China/market-specific)
- Buyback programs, dividend increases, capital returns
- Analyst upgrades or initiation with strong targets
- Sector/macro tailwinds (e.g. supply chain, policy, currency)

## Risks (downside) — include at least one **falsification criterion** per thesis
- Company-specific: margin pressure, competition, product failure, key-man risk, governance
- Macro/market: HK/China regulatory, geopolitics, sector cycle, currency (HKD/USD peg, RMB exposure)
- Financial: high gearing, cash burn, dilution, accounting red flags
- **Technical/sentiment:** overbought, crowded positioning, low liquidity
- **What specific fact, if it became true, would invalidate the BUY/HOLD thesis?** Write this explicitly — this is the falsification criterion.

## Outputs
- Catalyst list (with expected timing and materiality)
- Risk list (with likelihood × impact where sensible)
- Explicit falsification criteria for the thesis
- A net-read on the news/sentiment balance

## End Conditions

This phase is **complete** when ALL of the following are true:

1. ✅ ≥3 concrete catalysts identified with timing/materiality
2. ✅ ≥3 material risks identified
3. ✅ Each thesis has at least one explicit falsification criterion
4. ✅ News/sentiment balance summarized with source cites
5. ✅ Risks mapped to likelihood × impact
