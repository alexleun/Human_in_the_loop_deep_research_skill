# Phase 7: Synthesis Document

**Purpose:** Integrate all five analysis rounds into a coherent final document that combines viewpoints and presents novel research directions.

## Document Structure

### Executive Summary
- What was studied, how, key findings across all papers
- **`[[wikilink]]` references** to the most significant papers and entities

### Cross-Cultural Patterns
- What behaviors appear universal vs culture-specific
- Organized by behavior domain, with supporting evidence from comparison matrix (Round 2)
- **`#behavior/{domain}` tags** on each section
- **`[[wikilink]]` cells** linking to comparison matrix cells and finding entity notes

### Contradictions and Methodological Insights
- What remains unresolved and why
- Organized by contradiction pair with methodological analysis
- **`#status/contradicted` tags** and `[[wikilink]]` pairs

### Research Gaps and Blind Spots
- What is unknown, under-studied, or misrepresented
- Organized by population, region, methodology, and temporal dimension
- **`#status/gap` tags** and `[[behavior/{domain}]]` wikilinks

### Novel Research Directions
- Top 5-10 proposed research questions with:
  - Rationale (which gap/contradiction/pattern motivated it)
  - Suggested methodology
  - Expected impact
  - Novelty and feasibility assessment
- **`[[wikilink]]` references** back to the specific gap, contradiction, or finding that motivated each question

### Appendix
- Full paper list with links to appraisals
- Coverage report reference
- Comparison matrix reference
- Research question catalog

## Verification

- Every `[[wikilink]]` in the synthesis document must point to an actual file in the vault — run a broken-link check
- Every factual claim must trace to a verbatim quote (not a paraphrase) from a paper appraisal
- Tag taxonomy must be consistent with vault conventions (no ad-hoc tags)
