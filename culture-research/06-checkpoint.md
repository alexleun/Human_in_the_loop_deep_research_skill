# Phase 6: Iteration Checkpoint

**Purpose:** Review all five analysis rounds as a coherent arc before writing the final synthesis. Catch weak links, missing dimensions, and insufficient grounding.

## Procedure

1. **Coherence check** — Do the research questions (Round 5) logically follow from the gaps (Round 4), contradictions (Round 3), and patterns (Rounds 1-2)? Or do they feel disconnected or speculative?

2. **Grounding audit** — For each research question in Round 5, trace back: is there a clear evidence chain from collected papers → thematic categorization → gap/contradiction → question? Flag questions with insufficient grounding as **speculative** (not evidence-derived).

3. **Missing dimension scan** — Check systematically for dimensions that may have been overlooked across all rounds:
   - Gender
   - Age / generation
   - Socioeconomic class
   - Technology penetration / digital divide
   - Climate / geography
   - Urban vs rural
   - Seasonality
   - Life stage transitions
   - Disability / accessibility

4. **Loop decision** — If missing dimensions or weak links found:
   - Identify which round(s) need revisiting
   - Loop back to the relevant round, add the missing dimension, regenerate its output
   - Then re-run the checkpoint before proceeding

5. **Synthesis readiness** — If checkpoint passes (weak links minimal, no critical blind spots), proceed to Phase 7.

## Output

`knowledge-base/analysis/checkpoint-review.md` documenting:
- What was reviewed
- What passed / what was flagged
- Any loops taken and what changed
- Final readiness assessment: **proceed to synthesis** or **needs more work**
