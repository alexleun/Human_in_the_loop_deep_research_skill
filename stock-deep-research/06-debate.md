# Phase 6: Bull/Bear Debate & Catalysts — Adversarial Cases

**Who drives:** LLM runs two independent, strongest-standard cases; Human referees.

**Purpose:** Argue the **bullish** and **bearish** theses each at their strongest, then surface the **net catalysts and risks** and a **falsification criterion** — before any rating is reached (P5, P8).

## Procedure

1. **Generate inputs independently, in order:**
   - Feed the Bull the combined output of Phases 3–5. The Bull builds the strongest case: catalysts, what the market is missing, alignment with valuation/DCF/reverse-DCF.
   - Separately, feed the Bear the SAME inputs (independent, unbiased). The Bear leads with the strongest material risk (fraud/bankruptcy/sanctions/litigation, valuation, competition, execution, concentration, lookahead exposure).
   - Record each side as a standalone note (`debate/bull.md`, `debate/bear.md`) before any synthesis.
2. **Refutation pass:** cross-read the two notes; identify which Bull points the Bear refutes and vice-versa. Flag any claim that does not survive the strongest opposing argument.
3. **Catalysts & risks ledger:**
   - **Upside catalysts** (fundamental, event-driven, policy/Stock Connect, forex, macro) — each with timing, probability, and link to valuation.
   - **Downside risks** (same taxonomy) — pre-seed inputs for the fragility audit in Phase 7.
   - Identify what would **change the thesis** (falsification criteria, P8).
4. **Anti-lookahead** (P9): debates must run on pre-cutoff evidence; do not smuggle in post-decision outcomes to settle the debate.
5. **Stay objective-driven (P1 / P5):** frame "generate the strongest bull case" / "generate the strongest bear case" — do not assign personas or role-play.

## Output

`debate/bull.md`, `debate/bear.md`, a refutation matrix, and a catalysts/risks ledger with falsification criteria. These are the primary inputs to Phase 7 (fragility audit) and Phase 8 (synthesis + gates).

## End Conditions

This phase is **complete** when ALL of the following are true:

1. ✅ Bull and Bear cases are each written at their strongest, before any synthesis
2. ✅ A refutation matrix exists (each side's points tested against the strongest opposition)
3. ✅ Catalysts (upside) and risks (downside) ledger compiled with timing/probability/source
4. ✅ Falsification criteria are drafted (what would invalidate the thesis)
5. ✅ Debate uses only pre-cutoff (as-of ≤ decision date) evidence (P9)
6. ✅ No rating is yet stated — this phase produces inputs only
